# 🌌 The Aurora Dream

> **A journey to see the Northern & Southern Lights.**

**The Aurora Dream** is an interactive aurora travel-planning web experience covering destinations across the Northern and Southern Hemispheres.

It combines visual storytelling, destination research, an interactive map, seasonal planning, budget estimation, destination comparison, and interactive discovery tools into a single experience.

## ✨ Live Demo

[The Aurora Dream](https://pixel-perfect-snap-662.lovable.app/)

## 🌍 Overview

There are two auroras, two hemispheres, and countless possible journeys.

This project explores **12 destinations** where travellers may experience the Northern or Southern Lights:

### Northern Lights — Aurora Borealis

* 🇨🇦 Canada
* 🇫🇮 Finland
* 🇮🇸 Iceland
* 🇳🇴 Norway
* 🇸🇪 Sweden
* 🇺🇸 Alaska
* 🇬🇱 Greenland

### Southern Lights — Aurora Australis

* 🇦🇶 Antarctica
* 🇦🇺 Tasmania
* 🇳🇿 New Zealand
* 🇦🇷 Argentina
* 🇨🇱 Chile

The goal is not simply to list destinations, but to help users understand **where, when, why, and how** an aurora journey could be planned.

## 🚀 Features

### 🌌 Aurora Education

Learn how auroras form through an interactive explanation covering:

* Solar wind
* Earth's magnetic field
* Atmospheric collisions
* Aurora colours
* Northern and Southern auroral ovals

### 🗺️ Interactive World Map

Explore the 12 destinations geographically and distinguish between:

* Northern Lights destinations
* Southern Lights destinations
* Approximate auroral regions
* Destination locations

### 📍 Destination Explorer

Explore individual destinations with information such as:

* Aurora opportunity
* Recommended season
* Suggested number of nights
* Viewing areas
* Estimated trip costs

### 📅 Best Time to Go

Explore seasonal viewing windows and understand how darkness, latitude, weather, and the Earth's seasons affect aurora visibility.

### 💰 Cost Planner

Estimate travel costs based on factors including:

* Destination
* Trip duration
* Travel style
* Flights
* Accommodation
* Food
* Local transportation
* Visa / entry costs
* Aurora tours
* Activities
* Cold-weather equipment
* Travel insurance
* Connectivity
* Emergency buffer

The planner supports different travel styles such as budget, comfortable, and premium.

### ⚖️ Destination Comparison

Compare multiple destinations side by side to understand their differences in:

* Aurora opportunity
* Viewing season
* Cost
* Recommended duration
* Travel characteristics

### 🧭 Journey Planning

Interactive planning tools help transform general interest into a possible aurora journey.

### 📸 Photography

A dedicated visual section celebrates aurora photography and the landscapes surrounding the viewing experience.

### 📚 Sources

Research and reference information are included to make the planning content more transparent.

## 🎯 Project Goals

The project was created around four principles:

**Inspire**
Create an emotional connection with the idea of seeing an aurora.

**Educate**
Explain the science and geography behind the phenomenon.

**Compare**
Help visitors understand how destinations differ.

**Plan**
Turn inspiration into practical travel-planning information.

## 🧠 Important Data Note

The costs displayed by the project are **planning estimates**, not live prices.

Flight prices, hotels, exchange rates, visa requirements, weather conditions, aurora activity, and tour prices can change.

The application intentionally communicates this distinction and encourages users to verify current information before making travel or booking decisions.
